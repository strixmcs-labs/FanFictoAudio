# Version 1.5.0 — reading order, narration text controls, and faster AI narration

## Changes

**Create Audiobook** is split into **Voice & Text**, **Open Book**, and **Queue** panes. Sections can be dragged into a custom reading order, moved from a context menu, renamed, or restored to the EPUB order; orders and titles are saved with queue entries and used for M4B chapters and chapter files. Optional text controls skip author's notes and disclaimers, read chapter titles aloud where a section lacks one, and apply a global pronunciation list. Previews and estimates reflect the narrated text.

AI exports keep up to three OpenAI or two ElevenLabs requests in flight while encoding in reading order. Failed requests are still not retried automatically; passages other requests already returned are saved first, so a retry does not request them again. A queued book's saved settings are shown with a warning when they differ from the controls. The app notifies when exports or the queue finish in the background, supports dark mode, shows cover art, remembers AI speed/style and output format, restores the download list, and opens EPUBs from Finder.

## Requirements

Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Python, [FanFicFare](https://github.com/JimmXinu/FanFicFare), and a standalone FFmpeg/LAME encoder are bundled. FanFicFare, by Jim Miller (JimmXinu) and its contributors, powers story downloading, metadata, site adapters, and EPUB writing/updating. Provider keys stay in macOS Keychain.

## Compatibility and costs

Queues, per-book presets, and completed passages saved by 1.4 remain compatible. Up to three (OpenAI) or two (ElevenLabs) passages in progress when an export is cancelled may be sent and charged again. Provider rate limits under parallel requests were not tested with live accounts; a rate-limited export stops and can resume from completed passages. See the [User guide](../usage.md).
